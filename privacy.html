Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how AroundByte QRcode Studio handles information when you use our website and tools.

1. Information We Collect

AroundByte QRcode Studio is designed to let you create, customize, and scan QR codes and barcodes directly in your browser. We do not require you to create an account to use the core tools.

Information you enter into the tools, such as text, website addresses, contact details, or other data used to generate a code, is processed locally in your browser whenever the tool supports local processing. We do not intentionally collect or store this input on our servers.

2. Files and Local Processing

Files and images you select for use with our tools are processed in your browser where supported. They are not intentionally uploaded to our servers as part of the normal tool workflow.

Your generated QR codes and barcodes remain under your control. You can download or save the results using the options provided by your browser.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

Our website may use browser local storage to remember preferences such as language, theme, and other interface settings. This information stays in your browser and is not used by us to identify you personally.

You can clear this stored information through your browser settings. Doing so may reset your saved website preferences.

4. Advertising

We may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising partners, including Google AdSense. These partners may use cookies or similar technologies to display ads, measure advertising performance, and provide relevant advertising, subject to their own privacy policies and applicable settings.

You can learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services by visiting Google's advertising and privacy resources.

5. Third-Party Services and Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services. These external websites operate independently and have their own privacy policies and practices. We are not responsible for their content, security, or data-handling practices.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to maintain the website and protect its operation. However, no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should avoid entering confidential or sensitive information into QR codes or online tools unless you understand how that information will be used.

7. Children's Privacy

Our website is a general-purpose tool and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review the matter.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the website, its tools, or applicable requirements. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised date.
